RemerAI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

RemerAI (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal memory and relationship app. You write short notes about people you meet, and the app uses AI to help organize them into profiles, threads, reminders, and connections. This policy explains what we collect, how it’s used, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you use RemerAI, the following is stored in your account:

  • Account info — your email address (for sign-in) and authentication tokens.
  • Your entries — the journal/log text you write.
  • Derived data — people, threads (tags), groups, relationships, important dates, reminders, AI-generated summaries, and mood notes extracted from your entries.
  • Voice notes — if you use voice capture, audio is transcribed to text on your device; the transcript is saved as entry text.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or browsing activity. We do not sell your data.

How your data is used

  • To provide the core app: storing, organizing, and showing your memories.
  • To run AI features: when you save an entry, its text is sent to OpenAI to extract people, threads, dates, reminders, and to generate profile summaries and power semantic search.

Third parties who process your data

  • Supabase — our backend host. Stores your account and entries. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). Access is restricted per-user via row-level security.
  • OpenAI — processes your entry text to power the AI features described above. Per OpenAI’s API policy, data sent through the API is not used to train their models and is retained only briefly for abuse monitoring.

We only share what’s necessary for these services to function.

Security

  • All network traffic uses TLS 1.3.
  • Data at rest is encrypted (AES-256).
  • Row-level security ensures only you can access your own data.
  • Authentication tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain.

RemerAI is not end-to-end encrypted: because AI features require reading your entry text, our backend and OpenAI can process it. If this matters to you, keep especially sensitive details out of your entries.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account — Settings → “Delete account” permanently and immediately erases your account and all associated data (entries, people, threads, reminders). This cannot be undone.
  • Access / export — contact us (below) to request a copy of your data.
  • Correct — you can edit or delete any entry, person, thread, or reminder in the app at any time.

Children

RemerAI is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
