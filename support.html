Common questions

How do I write an entry?

Tap the + button and write the way you'd text a friend — “coffee with Lena, she just moved to Zurich and starts at Google in March.” No forms, no fields. The AI reads it and suggests people, threads, dates and follow-ups, and nothing is saved until you approve it.

Can I dictate instead of typing?

Yes. In the composer, tap the microphone, then press and hold while you speak and release when you're done. Hold again to add more — it appends rather than starting over. Speech is transcribed on your device.

The AI got a name or a detail wrong.

Everything it suggests is editable. Open the person and tap Edit to fix their name or add nicknames, or use the ⋯ → Merge with… menu if the same person ended up with two profiles. You can also decline any suggestion instead of accepting it.

Why does a profile say so little?

Profiles fill in as you write more about someone. Tap the ✨ button on a profile to have the AI re-read every entry that mentions them and rewrite the summary.

Which languages work for dictation?

Whatever your device supports — set it in Settings → Voice. That includes German (Switzerland), French (Switzerland) and Italian (Switzerland). Swiss German dialect is not supported by Apple's speech recognition, so dictating in Schwiizerdütsch won't transcribe well.

Does it work offline?

No. Entries are stored in your account and the AI features need a connection, so you'll see a banner and saving is blocked while you're offline.

I need larger text.

RemerAI follows your system text size — change it in iOS Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size, or Accessibility → Display & Text Size for the larger options.

How do I export my data?

Settings → Export my data produces a text file of everything you've written, which you can save or send anywhere.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. It permanently and immediately erases your account and everything in it — entries, people, threads, reminders. It cannot be undone.

Reminders aren't arriving.

Check that notifications are allowed for RemerAI in iOS Settings → Notifications. Reminders fire at the time set on the reminder, or at your default time from Settings → Reminder time.

Privacy

Entries are private to your account. To power the AI features, entry text is sent to OpenAI for processing — it is not used to train their models. The full details are in the privacy policy.
